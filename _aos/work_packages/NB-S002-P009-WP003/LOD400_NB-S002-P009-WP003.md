## 2. Confirmed root-cause (build commit 1 — highest impact)
**G-01 verified by team_100:** `assets/css/t1.css` opens with its `:root{…}` block **truncated** — orphan `--soil-wash-2/-know-wash/-code-wash` declarations with no `:root {` and a missing `--soil-wash`; parser discards them → all four world washes undefined → every world-page strata gradient flattens. (`t2.css` has the correct full `:root` — copy that pattern.) **Fix:** restore the full `:root` block (per DESIGN_GAP_ANALYSIS §2) at the top of `t1.css`. ~4 lines, fixes all world pages at once. This also clears the long-standing t1.css brace imbalance.
