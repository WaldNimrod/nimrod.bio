## D2 — Zero horizontal scroll @360 + @375 (AT-D13) — **FAIL**

| Viewport | `scrollWidth` | `clientWidth` | Equal? | Notes |
|----------|---------------|---------------|--------|-------|
| **360** | 372 | 360 | **no** (Δ **12px**) | Hero band contained (`hero-poster` sw=cw=360, `overflow-x: hidden/clip`) |
| **375** | 389 | 375 | **no** (Δ **14px**) | Same pattern |
| 768 | 768 | 768 | yes | — |
| 1440 | 1440 | 1440 | yes | — |

**Isolation (0.6.2):** Hiding `.t7-worlds` drops document `scrollWidth` to **360** (Δ12px removed). Hero hide does **not** change `scrollWidth`.  
**Root cause:** `.t7-worlds .wi-col::before` radial glow uses `inset: -8% -10%`, inflating `.worlds-intro` / `.t7-wrap` `scrollWidth` to **372** while layout boxes stay within the viewport.  
**Not a regression of the hero fix** — hero constraint works; remaining overflow is the worlds intro backdrop.

**Repro:** Load home @360 → `document.documentElement.scrollWidth > clientWidth` → slight horizontal pan possible.
