## 3. Page Goal

Write a T2 service page for **professional produce** from Nimrod’s hydroponic greenhouse.

This is not “order a vegetable box”.

The page should explain:

- What is grown and for whom.
- Why the produce is unusual / professional.
- Why this is based on commitment, seasonality, and fit — not volume sales.
- How a restaurant or serious kitchen should approach it.
- What “maximum one more restaurant” means without sounding desperate or salesy.

The page should remain a service page: clear, concrete, and easy to act on.

## 4. Facts Allowed

Use only these facts:

- Active hydroponic greenhouse.
- Greenhouse size: `420 מ״ר`.
- Most produce is for home.
- Part of the produce is sold to **one restaurant**.
- Approved restaurant name: `מסעדת המחתרת התאילנדית`.
- Approved restaurant crops:
  - `עירית שומית`
  - `פאטבונג` / `תרד מים תאילנדי`
- Produce is grown by prior arrangement / seasonal commitment.
- Positioning: not commodity vegetable boxes.
- It is a season-long fixed-price commitment for unique crops that justify the price.
- Approved phrase / concept: `זכות להיות לקוח של החממה`.
- “מקסימום עוד מסעדה אחת” is allowed only delicately, not as a sales declaration.

Do not:

- Invent other crops.
- Invent more restaurants.
- Write “מסעדות” as active plural unless framed as potential audience / future fit.
- Invent supply quantities, delivery schedule, yield, frequency, or price.
- Promise consistency beyond what is factually safe.
- Write customer names other than `מסעדת המחתרת התאילנדית`.
- Turn this into a general produce shop / CSA / vegetable-box page.

## 5. Tone And Positioning

Tone:

- Quiet confidence.
- Precise, warm, concrete.
- Anti-hard-sell.
- Not “premium/exclusive” marketing language.
- Not scarcity manipulation.

Sales posture:

> This is for the right fit.  
> If it fits — talk to me.  
> If not — no problem.

Conceptual cue:

Use at most one gentle cue from the ideology:

- seasonality
- commitment
- שיתופעולה with the crop / greenhouse / kitchen rhythm
- human scale

Do not explain the worldview.

Avoid:

- CDIP
- אנטרופיה
- נגנטרופיה
- רקורסיה
- Unless
- spiritual-depth terms
- SaaS/corporate/buzzwords

`פרמקלצר` is probably not needed on this page. If used, it must be historically/operationally grounded, not a label.
