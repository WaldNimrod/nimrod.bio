## 2 · Voice system — 3 registers

The site speaks in three registers. Pick by context.

| Register | When | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| **מארח (Host)** | field, community, activities, CTA, blog | warm "אתם", measured "!", practical & exact (hours/sizes/prices), warm invite, honest aside |
| **חושב (Thinker)** | thesis, bridges, digital, about | em-dash pivot, "מבחינתי", lists, forward-pointing close, unsoftened self-criticism |
| **מלח (Sailor)** | reflective long-form / blog / journey only | staccato line-per-clause, breath-over-head, "real vs. marketed", concession-pivot at climax |

**Says:** מבחינתי · חשוב לי · "— אבל" · ועוד · גשר · בלגן שתמיד יש אצלי · service לא יזמית
**Never says:** disruption · game-changer · סקייל · "הפלטפורמה שלנו" · "אנחנו מאמינים" · AI-first · "הפתרון שחיפשתם" · כידוע · חדשנות פורצת-דרך

Full reference: `nimrod-book/profiles/03_PROFILE_VOICE_AND_LANGUAGE.md` (v1.1).

## 3 · The thesis (context only — NOT to be quoted on site)

Everything is a complex system; the same patterns recur at different scale. A closed
system tends to disorder; you open it by **connection** or by a **reset (BOOM)**;
then a **line becomes a circle** that rolls on its own → *"מספיק לכולם לתמיד"*. The
choice that starts it is **Unless** (Dr. Seuss / The Lorax: what to do with the seed).
**Unless is the site tagline — it stands alone, never explained.**
Background: `nimrod-book/chapters/09_CLOSED_SYSTEMS_BOOM_AND_THE_CIRCLE.md`.
