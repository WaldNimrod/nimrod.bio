## A · The unifying thesis — LOCKED (this is the spine of the site)

Stated by the owner in his own words. **CDIP expressed as a way of acting.**

- **Master lens — permaculture design.** Everything is a complex system; the same
  patterns recur at different scale. A garden (insects *and* workers/customers), a
  software company (relationships, desires, expenses), vibe coding — same patterns.
- **Law:** a **closed** system tends toward disorder. Goal = **cooperate with
  nature**, not defeat it — find its patterns and flow with them. (= ecology +
  environmental preservation.)
- **Two ways out:** **BOOM** (reset — break out of routine / ordinary thinking) OR
  **bridges** (connections / mutual feedings between systems).
- **Result:** line → **circle**; the circle rolls (מתגלגל), is natural, works →
  **"מספיק לכולם לתמיד"** (first garden slogan).
- **Unless** = the choice from *The Lorax* (Dr. Seuss): what to do with the seed →
  choose to act differently → that is **"קטן זה יפה"**.

### Copy discipline (do NOT violate)
1. **Demonstrate, never name.** No "entropy / recursion / CDIP / permaculture" as
   label-terms in surface copy. Show a system closing, a connection opening it, the
   circle rolling. Reader infers.
2. **Line → circle, not line → order.**
3. **Cooperate, don't conquer.**
4. **Products on the way, never the headline.** Open on a connection, close on a product.
5. **Unless stands alone** (tagline only). The Lorax/seed/entropy lineage → a
   dedicated **post series + visual**, never inline. (Owner has NotebookLM research
   + a Facebook post series to supply as background — mining pending.)
